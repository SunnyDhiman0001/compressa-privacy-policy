# CompressA Privacy Policy


**App:** CompressA (Android package `com.compressa.app`)
**Developer:** Sunny Dhiman
**Contact:** sd161928@gmail.com

CompressA is an offline, privacy-first app for compressing images and working with PDFs. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## 1. Summary

- CompressA works entirely on your device.
- We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your images, PDFs, or personal information.
- The app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.
- The app does not use the network to process your files.

## 2. Information we collect

**None.** CompressA does not collect personal data, and we have no servers that receive your files or usage data.

## 3. How your files are handled

- Images and PDFs you choose are read and processed locally on your device.
- Compressed or edited results are saved to your device (for example your gallery or app storage) or shared only when you choose to, using Android's share sheet.
- Files are never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app.
- Image metadata (such as location) is accessed only on-device as part of processing. It is not transmitted anywhere.

## 4. Data stored on your device

CompressA stores a small amount of data locally:

- **History** of your compression/edit jobs (file names, sizes, dates) so you can review them.
- **Settings**, such as theme and whether onboarding has been completed.

This data stays on your device. You can clear it from the app's History screen, by clearing app storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## 5. Permissions

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Read media / storage (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 12 and below) | To let you pick images from your gallery. |
| Write storage (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, Android 9 and below) | To save compressed images to your gallery on older Android versions. |
| Access media location (`ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION`) | To read or preserve image location metadata during processing. Never sent off-device. |

Files you pick through the system file picker are accessed only for the file you select.

## 6. Third-party services

CompressA does not integrate advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. It uses open-source libraries for on-device image and PDF processing, picking, and sharing. These libraries run locally and do not send your data to us.

When you share a file, the app you share it to (for example a messaging app) has its own privacy policy.

## 7. Children's privacy

CompressA does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 8. Security

Because your files never leave your device, they are protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). We recommend keeping your device updated and locked.

## 9. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. You control all app data on your device and can remove it at any time (see section 4).

## 10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example if a feature requires network access), we will update this policy and the effective date above, and in-app notice will be given where appropriate.
